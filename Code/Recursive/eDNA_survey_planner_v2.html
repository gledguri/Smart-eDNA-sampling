Exploratory manuscript calibration. The default coefficients come from the main simulation fit using 2,000, 1,000, 600, 358, 330, 300, 260, 220, and 150 samples, compared with the 4,000-sample fitted reference map. The simulation used 350 internally and represented it as 358 when calculating density to match the empirical survey. This is the 4,000-reference analysis, not the separate analysis benchmarked against the 358-sample reconstruction. ε is RMSE on the natural-log response scale; it is not percent error, detection probability, or a confidence level. The equation now calculates beyond the fitted density range of 1.5–20 samples per 10,000 km² per stratum; those results are clearly marked as extrapolations.

How to read the result

—

If every station supplies every stratum, the required station count equals the samples per stratum. The total shown above counts all site-stratum observations. Filters, PCR replicates, and repeated visits are separate effort types.

The equation used is log(ε) = ω + β log(E) + θ log(ρ) + γ log(E) log(ρ). Its fitted support is ρ = 25–1,000 km and E = 1.5–20 samples per 10,000 km² per stratum. Calculations outside the E range extend the fitted formula and carry an extrapolation warning.

Interpretation. The bounds are exp(log(C) − ε) and exp(log(C) + ε), assuming the true concentration was at 100 copies/L. The curve assumes normally distributed error in log space, producing a right-skewed lognormal density in copies/L. The bounds show ±1 ε (about 68% only under that assumption); they are not absolute biological minima and maxima.
