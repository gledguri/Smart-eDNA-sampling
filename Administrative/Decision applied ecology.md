We have recently launched a category for "Method" papers that present novel and innovative tools, approaches, or techniques to carry out research in applied ecology and I feel your paper would make a good fit for this manuscript type with some revision. Papers in the Method section present novel and innovative tools, approaches, or techniques to carry out research in applied ecology. Method papers should explicitly discuss the potential applications of the work on data collection, policy, management, or the analysis and solution of major environmental problems. The main text sections of a Method paper generally consist of an Introduction, a Description of the new method, a Case Study, a Discussion, and a Perspectives and Applications summary section. The paper may contain tables and figures. Specific implementation details or other instructions or guidelines may be provided as Supporting Information.
